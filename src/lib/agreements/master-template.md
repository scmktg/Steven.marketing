## SCHEDULE B — Our working terms

### 1. Working together
We research, plan, run and improve the marketing work in Schedule A, and share clear reports. We act with care, but cannot promise specific sales or rankings. The Client provides accurate business information, reasonable account access and timely approvals, and remains responsible for its products, claims, fulfilment and legal compliance. We agree before spending the Client's money. Advertising and other approved third-party costs are separate from our commission.

### 2. How commission works
A contract month runs from the start-date anniversary to the day before the next one. **Growth** is the revenue defined in Schedule A minus the fixed monthly baseline, or zero if lower. Each tier rate applies **only to growth within that tier**. If another fee model is written in Schedule A, that agreed model applies instead. The revenue definition and baseline must use a consistent basis.

We provide the sales figures and calculation with each invoice. Refunds and reporting corrections are adjusted openly, without counting anything twice. The Client provides reasonable records needed to verify results. We invoice after each contract month; invoices are payable within 14 days. GST is added only where applicable.

### 3. Accounts, work and information
The Client keeps ownership and control of its business accounts, customer data, brand and paid-for marketing work. Steven Marketing keeps its reusable tools, methods and templates, but the Client may continue using any parts included in paid-for work. We both protect private business information and comply with applicable privacy laws. Neither side may block reasonable access to the Client's own accounts.

### 4. Ending the agreement
Either side can end the agreement after the initial commitment with 30 days' written notice. The end date is the first contract-month end at least 30 days after notice. Either side may end sooner if the other seriously breaks an agreed term and does not fix it within 14 days of written notice. We hand over paid-for work and remove our access when the engagement ends. All properly earned fees up to that date remain payable. There is **no separate cancellation penalty**.

### 5. Fair payment for results after we finish
Some marketing work keeps bringing in business after we stop. If the Client ends the agreement normally, or we end it because the Client has not fixed a serious breach, a **limited commission** may continue for up to three contract months.

It applies **only** where the work identified in Schedule A remains in use or has a reasonably evidenced continuing benefit. We use the agreed revenue rules and baseline, excluding clearly unrelated sales or new work done independently. Each month's qualifying commission is then multiplied by **100%, 75%, then 50%**. The total cannot exceed the **maximum agreed in Schedule A**; if no maximum is agreed, **no post-termination commission applies**. We share the supporting figures and discuss any disagreement before invoicing disputed amounts.

This payment ends after those three months. It does not stop the Client using its own systems or engaging another agency. It does not apply if the Client ended the agreement because **we** failed to fix a serious breach. Fees already earned before the end date remain payable.

### 6. Keeping things fair
Both sides will act reasonably, raise problems early and try to resolve disagreements together before using mediation or the courts of the state or territory in Schedule A. Neither side may change signed terms alone. We are each responsible for our own conduct; nothing removes rights that Australian law does not allow us to remove. Any change to this agreement must be accepted by both sides in writing.
